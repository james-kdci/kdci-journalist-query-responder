## Tone/voice notes

**Confidence: HIGH — derived from 3 real, substantial sample responses** (`writing-samples/2026-07-30-sample-query-responses.md`), not guessed.

- **Balanced/dialectic structure, not absolutist.** Consistently presents both sides before landing a view — "rarely a straight choice," "generally," "most of the time" — then converges on a measured recommendation rather than picking one extreme. Rarely says something is simply good or bad.
- **Distills to a clean reduction at the end.** Habitually closes a complex explanation with a compact "it comes down to X" or "at its simplest, this is three things" — a memorable summary line after the nuance, not instead of it.
- **First-person conviction used sparingly, at judgment moments only.** "The way I see it...", "I read these moves primarily as...", "I would not expect...", "The point I would most want a business to hear is..." — signals personal opinion deliberately, not throughout.
- **Root-causes failure to people/process, not the technology.** E.g. "when these projects fail, the cause is rarely the model itself — it usually traces back to unclear goals and weak oversight." Consistent instinct to defend the tool and interrogate the deployment.
- **Names real companies and tools directly and comfortably** — Microsoft, OpenAI, Google, Meta, Anthropic, Delphi, ElevenLabs, HeyGen all named plainly with no hedging. No apparent reluctance to be specific/attributable.
- **Grounds abstract claims in measurable, traceable terms.** When asked how to judge an AI agent's value, answers in concrete metrics (response speed, resolution rate, tone consistency, accuracy against company standards, customer approval rating) rather than general enthusiasm.
- **Uses structured, labeled sub-answers for multi-part queries** (bolded lead-in phrases per sub-question, e.g. "**Where traditional automation still wins.**") rather than one flowing paragraph — makes each sub-question easy to lift as a standalone quote.
- **Technical fluency translated back to plain English every time** — comfortable with context windows, RAG-style retrieval, orchestration layers, but always closes the loop back to what it means for a business reader, never leaves a claim at the jargon level.
